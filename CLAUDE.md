# 400a

Materials for EDUC 400A (research design and quantitative methods, Stanford GSE).

## Pre-class prompts

For any task involving a pre-class prompt (writing, revising or reviewing one), follow `PRECLASS_PROMPT_PROTOCOL.md`. `histogram_prompt.txt` is the style model.

Students have links to these files. Never rename, move or change the extension of an existing prompt. Revisions are edits in place.
